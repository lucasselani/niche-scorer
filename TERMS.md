## Derived metrics

All scores and indicators produced by Niche Scorer are generated independently by the tool and are not sourced from or endorsed by YouTube.

## Data retention

API responses are cached for a maximum of 24 hours. Public statistics and derived metrics may be retained for up to 36 months only if YouTube grants the additional derived-metrics and data-storage permissions; all other data (such as video titles and channel names) is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as described in PRIVACY.md.
